READING

Scaling v in proportion to the round's own bundle price changes nothing:
all twelve rounds keep the recommendation the flat model gives. Linear
scaling is also harmless. The recommendation only starts to move when the
early rounds are discounted very steeply. At (k/6)^2, which values a
group-stage match at $315, three rounds become Stay home; at (k/6)^3, four
do. One further round changes at (k/6)^2, Norway's Round of 16, and it
changes to Wait by $4.33, which is a tie rather than a finding.

So the flat v is safe unless the fan values an early-round trip at a small
fraction of a Final, and if he does, the consequence is that he should not
travel to those rounds at all rather than that he should book them
differently. No scheme moves the Final, which is the round the report's
headline figures are drawn from.
